**Gym Members Report | Power BI Dashboard**

Project Summary:
Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze gym members' fitness and workout data. The dashboard provides insights into workout frequency, calories burned, BMI, heart rate, hydration, and member demographics, helping identify fitness trends and support data-driven decision-making.

Key Features
Built interactive dashboards with slicers for Experience Level filtering.
Analyzed workout frequency across Strength, Cardio, Yoga, and HIIT.
Visualized calorie expenditure by workout session duration.
Compared average age by gender.
Monitored health metrics including BMI, Body Fat Percentage, Weight, Height, Water Intake, and Heart Rate (Average, Resting, Maximum BPM).
Created dynamic charts including bar charts, pie charts, donut charts, KPI cards, and combination charts.
Improved data visualization using customized themes and interactive filters.
Key Insights
Strength Training had the highest workout participation.
Longer workout sessions resulted in higher calorie burn.
Average member age was approximately 38–39 years across both genders.
Experienced members demonstrated better cardiovascular efficiency.
Water intake showed a positive relationship with overall fitness indicators.
BMI remained relatively consistent across different workout types.

Business Value

The dashboard helps gym owners and fitness managers monitor member performance, identify workout trends, personalize fitness plans, and make informed business decisions to improve member engagement and retention.
